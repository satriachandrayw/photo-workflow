**PROFESSIONAL PORTRAIT ANALYSIS FOR CORPORATE HEADSHOT GENERATION**
Purpose: Analyze portrait photographs to create detailed subject descriptions that will inform the generation of premium corporate headshots. Your analysis will serve as the foundation for AI-generated professional portraits suitable for business websites, LinkedIn profiles, executive directories, and corporate marketing materials.

**ANALYSIS SCOPE & PARAMETERS:**
- Input: Single-subject portrait photograph (any framing from headshot to full-body)
- Focus: Comprehensive human subject analysis excluding environmental elements
- Output: Rich, flowing narrative description capturing appearance, characteristics, and professional presence
- Special Requirement: Full-body images require detailed pose, stance, and body language analysis

**ANALYSIS EXCLUSIONS:**
Concentrate exclusively on the human subject. Do not analyze or describe:
- Environmental elements, backgrounds, or settings
- Lighting conditions, camera angles, or photographic composition
- Technical photography aspects or image quality

**STEP-BY-STEP ANALYSIS FRAMEWORK:**

**STEP 1: INITIAL SUBJECT ASSESSMENT**
Conduct a comprehensive overview analysis covering:
- Apparent age range and demographic presentation
- Overall physical presence and professional bearing
- First impression of confidence level and approachability
- General build and proportional characteristics

**STEP 2: DETAILED FACIAL FEATURE ANALYSIS**
Execute precise facial characteristic documentation:
- Skin tone analysis: undertones, clarity, texture, and natural luminosity
- Facial geometry: shape classification (oval, round, square, heart, diamond)
- Eye characteristics: shape, size, color depth, and iris patterns
- Eyebrow architecture: thickness, arch definition, natural growth patterns
- Nose structure: bridge width, nostril shape, tip definition, and proportional balance
- Lip analysis: fullness, natural color, curve definition, and symmetry
- Jawline and cheekbone prominence: definition, angles, and structural impact
- Facial hair patterns: growth, styling, density, and professional appropriateness

**STEP 3: HAIR AND STYLING DOCUMENTATION**
Comprehensive hair presentation analysis:
- Cut style and length specifications with professional terminology
- Color analysis: base tones, highlights, natural variations, and professional appearance
- Texture classification: straight, wavy, curly, coarse, fine, or mixed textures
- Styling approach: natural, structured, casual, or formal presentation
- Hairline definition and natural growth patterns
- Overall hair health and professional maintenance level

**STEP 4: ACCESSORIES AND PROFESSIONAL APPROPRIATENESS EVALUATION**
Systematic accessory assessment with corporate suitability analysis:
- Professional accessories: prescription eyewear, elegant jewelry, cultural or religious items worn with dignity
- Non-corporate items requiring attention: casual headphones, wireless earbuds, informal lanyards, face masks, smoking-related items, overly casual jewelry
- Style enhancement elements that support professional presentation
- Items that may detract from corporate portrait effectiveness

**STEP 5: PHYSICAL PRESENCE AND BODY LANGUAGE ANALYSIS**
Comprehensive posture and presence evaluation:
- Shoulder positioning: width, alignment, and confidence indicators
- Posture analysis: spine alignment, head posture alignment, and professional bearing
- Build assessment: proportional characteristics and fitness indicators
- For full-body images: detailed stance analysis, arm positioning, leg placement, weight distribution, and overall body language communication
- Professional presence indicators and personality expression through physical positioning

**STEP 6: DISTINCTIVE CHARACTERISTICS IDENTIFICATION**
Unique feature documentation for portrait recreation:
- Memorable facial features that define the subject's appearance
- Characteristic expressions or natural tendencies
- Distinguishing marks, features, or asymmetries that add character
- Elements that make the subject immediately recognizable and professionally memorable

**OUTPUT FORMAT REQUIREMENTS:**
Generate a cohesive, flowing narrative description integrating all analysis steps into professional photographer's notes. Structure as a rich, descriptive prose that naturally incorporates all required elements while maintaining readability and professional tone.

**PROFESSIONAL NARRATIVE EXAMPLE:**
"This subject appears to be in their late twenties with an oval facial structure and smooth, medium-light brown skin with warm undertones. Their almond-shaped eyes are deep brown with subtle amber flecks, framed by naturally arched eyebrows of medium density that follow the orbital bone structure.

Their hair features a contemporary cut with subtle layering, rich black coloration, and natural straight texture. The hairline presents clean, well-defined edges with natural growth patterns suitable for professional portraiture.

Facial features include a straight, proportionally balanced nose with refined tip definition, medium-full lips with natural coloration, and a defined jawline. Prominent cheekbones create facial contours that enhance portrait lighting.

No accessories are present - no jewelry, eyewear, headphones, wireless earbuds, lanyards, or other non-corporate elements that would require removal for professional portrait generation.

Physical presence shows proportionate build with broad shoulder structure. Posture assessment reveals upright spine alignment with controlled positioning. The visible torso from mid-chest framing shows proportionate muscle tone.

The subject's most distinctive characteristic is their naturally asymmetric smile, combined with defined jawline architecture and prominent cheekbone structure, creating a memorable face suitable for corporate portraiture."

**PROFESSIONAL WRITING STANDARDS:**

**Language and Specificity Requirements:**
- Use precise, direct descriptive language with professional photography terminology
- Provide specific, measurable details rather than generic descriptions (e.g., "deep brown eyes with amber undertones" versus "brown eyes")
- Apply professional portrait analysis vocabulary including facial geometry and structural assessments
- Maintain objective, observational tone with concise, focused descriptions

**Content Accuracy and Scope:**
- Document only observable visual characteristics without inferring personality traits, cultural background, emotional states, or subjective interpretations
- When uncertain about specific features, use professional qualifying language ("appears to demonstrate," "suggests," "indicates")
- Provide sufficient technical detail to enable accurate portrait recreation by AI generation systems
- Focus exclusively on elements relevant to professional corporate portrait generation

**Narrative Structure and Flow:**
- Integrate all six analysis steps into a cohesive, direct narrative that reads as professional photographer's technical notes
- Include full-body pose and body language analysis when applicable
- Maintain logical progression from general impression through detailed feature analysis to distinctive characteristics
- Ensure each sentence contributes essential information for corporate portrait generation

**Professional Application Focus:**
- Emphasize features and characteristics that translate effectively to corporate photography
- Highlight elements that enhance professional presentation while noting items requiring adjustment
- Provide analysis depth sufficient for premium corporate headshot generation
- Structure information to support consistent, high-quality AI portrait generation results
